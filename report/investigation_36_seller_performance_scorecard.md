# Investigation Title

**Investigation 36 – Seller Performance Scorecard: Developing a Multi-KPI Framework for Evaluating Marketplace Seller Performance**

---

# Business Context

An e-commerce marketplace's success depends heavily on the performance of its sellers. While revenue is often used as the primary measure of seller success, relying on a single financial metric provides an incomplete assessment of overall performance. Sellers generating high sales may simultaneously experience poor customer satisfaction, delayed deliveries, or limited product diversity, ultimately affecting the customer experience and the marketplace's reputation.

To support data-driven decision-making, marketplace management requires a balanced framework capable of evaluating sellers across multiple operational and financial dimensions. Such a framework enables consistent seller benchmarking, supports performance monitoring, identifies operational improvement opportunities, and informs strategic partnership decisions.

This investigation develops a comprehensive Seller Performance Scorecard that integrates several key performance indicators (KPIs) into a single weighted Seller Performance Index, providing management with a holistic view of seller effectiveness.

---

# Business Objectives

The objectives of this investigation were to:

- Calculate the total revenue generated by each seller.
- Measure operational activity through completed order volume.
- Evaluate customer satisfaction using review scores.
- Assess delivery reliability through on-time delivery performance.
- Measure seller product diversity.
- Standardize multiple KPIs onto a common scoring scale.
- Develop a weighted Seller Performance Index.
- Classify sellers into meaningful business performance categories.
- Produce an executive-ready dataset suitable for dashboard integration and strategic reporting.

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The Seller Performance Scorecard was developed using five business KPIs selected to represent both financial and operational performance.

| KPI | Weight | Business Purpose |
|------|--------:|------------------|
| Total Revenue | 35% | Measures financial contribution |
| Orders Fulfilled | 25% | Measures operational activity |
| Average Review Score | 20% | Measures customer satisfaction |
| On-Time Delivery Rate | 15% | Measures operational reliability |
| Product Diversity | 5% | Measures product portfolio breadth |

The weighting model prioritizes revenue generation while ensuring that customer experience and operational excellence remain significant contributors to overall seller performance.

---

# Analytical Approach

The investigation followed a structured analytical workflow.

## 1. KPI Development

Individual SQL queries were developed to calculate each KPI independently.

These included:

- Revenue aggregation
- Order fulfilment counts
- Average customer review scores
- Delivery performance calculations
- Product diversity metrics

---

## 2. KPI Standardization

Because the KPIs were measured using different units (currency, counts, ratings, and percentages), each metric was standardized onto a comparable 0–100 scale before weighting.

The following standardization methods were applied:

- Revenue → `NTILE(100)`
- Orders Fulfilled → `NTILE(100)`
- Product Diversity → `NTILE(100)`
- Review Score → `(Average Review ÷ 5) × 100`
- On-Time Delivery Rate → Existing percentage

This standardization ensured that no individual KPI dominated the final score solely due to differences in measurement scale.

---

## 3. Weighted Performance Model

A weighted scoring model was developed to combine the standardized KPIs into a single Seller Performance Score.

The final score was calculated as:

```text
Seller Performance Score =
(Revenue × 35%)
+
(Orders × 25%)
+
(Reviews × 20%)
+
(Delivery × 15%)
+
(Product Diversity × 5%)
```

The resulting score ranges from **0 to 100**, allowing consistent comparison across all sellers.

---

## 4. Seller Classification

Sellers were categorized using business-defined performance thresholds.

| Performance Score | Classification |
|------------------:|----------------|
| 90–100 | Elite Seller |
| 75–89.99 | High Performer |
| 60–74.99 | Strong Performer |
| 45–59.99 | Average Performer |
| Below 45 | Needs Improvement |

These classifications simplify interpretation for management and provide a practical framework for operational decision-making.

The Seller Performance Scorecard provides management with a practical decision-support tool rather than a simple ranking.

The framework enables the organization to:

- Identify high-performing strategic sellers.
- Detect sellers requiring operational support.
- Monitor customer satisfaction alongside revenue generation.
- Recognize sellers delivering consistent operational excellence.
- Evaluate seller performance objectively using standardized business rules.

Rather than relying solely on revenue, management gains a multidimensional understanding of seller quality.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical framework developed in this investigation, the following recommendations are proposed.

## 1. Establish a Preferred Seller Programme

Use the **Elite Seller** classification to identify candidates for premium marketplace benefits, promotional campaigns, and strategic partnerships.

## 2. Monitor Underperforming Sellers

Regularly review sellers classified as **Needs Improvement** to identify recurring operational issues, such as delayed deliveries or poor customer reviews.

## 3. Balance Financial and Operational KPIs

Avoid evaluating seller success solely by revenue. Operational reliability and customer satisfaction should remain key performance considerations.

## 4. Integrate the Scorecard into Executive Dashboards

The Seller Performance Index can serve as a reusable metric within Business Intelligence dashboards to support ongoing marketplace monitoring.

## 5. Periodically Review KPI Weights

Business priorities may change over time. The weighting framework should therefore be reviewed periodically to ensure alignment with organizational objectives.

---

# Limitations

Several limitations should be acknowledged:

- KPI weights were determined using analytical judgment rather than organizational policy.
- Revenue does not account for seller profitability.
- Product diversity measures breadth rather than product quality.
- Review scores may be influenced by relatively small sample sizes for some sellers.
- External operational factors such as logistics providers were not considered.
